XAirDrop 1.2 — Unpatched Mi Share test / code130
Public test prerelease. IPA DISABLED — DIAGNOSTIC.

DEVICE / ROM
Xiaomi 15 Ultra (xuanyuan): OS4.0.0.18.XOACNXM.
Xiaomi 15 (dada): OS4.0.0.61.XOCCNXM.
Exact kernel: 6.6.118-android15-8-gb9cc6ec16bc8-abogki536571621-4k.
The installer verifies device/ROM/kernel and the stock WLAN/mac80211 file and
loaded driver identities. Unsupported identities are rejected before changes.
The Xiaomi 15 profile has been added; physical testing on that device is pending.

REQUIREMENTS
KernelSU/SukiSU with preinit support. Original Mi Share 5.4.2 or 5.4.4 installed.
The ZIP does not include, modify or bind-mount Mi Share. Required network
permissions are granted through PRODUCT signature XML and checked at runtime.
The separate MiIntentResolver Gallery patch remains. No LSPosed/Xposed/hooks.

INSTALL
Back up the phone. Disable old XAirDrop and reboot with the stock driver.
Install the ZIP through KernelSU/SukiSU and reboot through the normal power menu.
Do not combine it with other AirDrop/WLAN modules.

START / CLOSE
Enable Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, then open Mi Share's Apple-device sharing screen.
You do not need to reinstall or press Action for every session. On the iPhone,
set AirDrop to Everyone for 10 Minutes. Start with a small photo, then test the
opposite direction separately. Close the Apple screen normally and check Wi-Fi.

TEST STATUS
Tested on Xiaomi 15 Ultra with original Mi Share 5.4.4: code129 passed two active
sessions lasting 62.92/62.60 seconds, normal close, Wi-Fi/IP/ping recovery and
automatic startup after a normal system reboot. Code130 adds the device profile,
messages and notes; driver/FW/XOS and session/shutdown code are identical.
Code130 was not separately installed on a phone. 82 offline checks passed.
iPhone discovery/sending/receiving on this unpatched variant: NOT TESTED.
Older patched-version transfer results do not establish support for this variant.

RECOVERY / LOGS
Disable the module and reboot if problems occur. Do not unload the candidate
driver live or blindly delete blocked/pending protection records. If boot gets
stuck, power off and boot while holding Volume Down; disable the module in
SukiSU safe mode.
Root terminal: su -c "sh /data/adb/modules/xairdrop/collect-log.sh"
Send Download/xairdrop-1.2-test-*.log privately with the direction of transfer.
